# My Stepping Stones into ESP32s

A collection of ESP32 Arduino sketches from roughly two months of hands-on experimentation.

**Gold to some, trash to others.**

This is a large dump of projects — some broken, most finished. If you sift through the good `.ino` folders, it should be worthwhile.

## What's in here
- Various ESP32 experiments and prototypes
- Mix of complete and incomplete sketches
- Learning notes, dead ends, and working examples from the journey

Feel free to dig around, take what works, and ignore the rest.
